Planet Facts

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun. A year on Mercury is just 88 Earth days.

Diameter4,879 km

Venus

Earth's "sister planet" — similar in size but shrouded in thick, toxic clouds. Hottest planet.

Diameter12,104 km

Earth

Our home — the only known planet with liquid water on its surface and life.

Diameter12,756 km

Mars

The Red Planet — home to the tallest mountain in the solar system (Olympus Mons).

Diameter6,792 km

Jupiter

The largest planet — its Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth that has raged for centuries.

Diameter142,984 km

Saturn

Famous for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock particles.

Diameter120,536 km

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates on its side — its axial tilt is 98°.

Diameter51,118 km

Neptune

The windiest planet — gusts reach 2,100 km/h. It's the farthest planet from the Sun.

Diameter49,528 km
